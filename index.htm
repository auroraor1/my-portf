About the Atelier

Photography, imagined.

Atelier Lucide creates AI-generated imagery and video for objects that deserve to be seen somewhere they've never physically been — a beach at dawn, an heirloom under museum glass, light no camera could wait for.

The studio isn't built around one category. Fragrance, jewelry, footwear, and whatever comes next — each series starts with a single object and a mood, then is built out frame by frame until it feels inevitable.
